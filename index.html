About

Between theory and the real thing.

Security is what happens when you take a system seriously enough to ask how it fails.

I study Informatics in a dual programme: semesters at DHBW Mannheim alternate with practical phases at ABB. That rhythm suits me — theory in the lecture hall, then the same ideas under real constraints, with real consequences.

My interests pull toward security — penetration testing, cryptography, the way small oversights compound into real risk. But I care about computer science as a whole: algorithms, systems close to the hardware, and code that is a pleasure to read.

Before Mannheim I grew up in Konstanz, on the lake. I still think best near water.

Interactive physics · Web

PhysicsLab

A browser-based collection of ten physics simulations spanning waves, acoustics, mechanics, oscillations and chaos. Each module runs a genuine numerical model: a wave tank that shows diffraction and Huygens' principle, a double pendulum tipping into chaos, a Lorenz attractor you can rotate in 3D, an N-body gravity sandbox where you place stars by hand and watch orbits form.

Every parameter is adjustable in real time, so abstract equations turn into intuition. The project demonstrates numerical simulation, real-time visualisation, and the discipline of making complex systems feel calm and legible.

  • Numerical simulation
  • Real-time visualisation
  • Chaos & dynamics
  • Interaction design
